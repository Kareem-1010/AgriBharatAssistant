# ICAR Guidelines: Cotton Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Pink Bollworm Control

## 1. Crop Context and Major Insect Pests
Cotton is India's principal commercial fiber crop. The major challenges during Kharif are sucking pests in early vegetative stages and bollworms in reproductive stages.

## 2. Pink Bollworm (PBW - *Pectinophora gossypiella*) Management
Pink Bollworm attacks flowers, squares, and developing bolls. Larvae feed inside bolls on seeds and lint, leaving double seeds and stained fiber.

### Monitoring & Economic Threshold Level (ETL):
- Install **Pheromone Traps** with Gossyplure @ 2 traps/acre at 45 days after sowing (DAS) for monitoring.
- **ETL**: 8 moths caught per trap per night for 3 consecutive nights OR 10% rosetted flowers / tender bolls showing larval presence.

### Non-Chemical & Cultural Measures:
- Mass trapping: Install 15-20 pheromone traps per acre.
- Destruction of rosetted flowers manually.
- Release egg parasitoid *Trichogramma bactrae* @ 60,000 to 1,00,000 parasites/acre at weekly intervals starting from 45 DAS.

### Chemical IPM Protocol (Staged Response):
1. **At Window 1 (60-90 DAS, Early Squaring/Flowering)**:
   - Spray Neem oil 1500 ppm @ 1 Litre/acre or NSKE 5% (Neem Seed Kernel Extract) @ 10 kg/acre.
2. **At Window 2 (90-120 DAS, Boll Formation)**:
   - If ETL crossed: Spray Spinosad 45% SC @ 75 ml in 200 L water per acre OR Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 88-100 g/acre.
3. **At Window 3 (120+ DAS, Late Stage/High Infestation)**:
   - Spray Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 500-600 ml/acre or Profenophos 50% EC @ 400-500 ml/acre.
   - *Safety Warning*: Strictly do not spray synthetic pyrethroids repeatedly to prevent resurgence of whitefly.

## 3. Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*) & Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV)
- **Damage**: Whitefly sucks sap and excretes honeydew, causing sooty mold and acting as vector for Cotton Leaf Curl Virus.
- **ETL**: 6-8 whiteflies (nymphs/adults) per leaf.
- **Control**:
  - Install yellow sticky traps @ 10-15 per acre at canopy height.
  - Initial stage: Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) or Azadirachtin 10,000 ppm @ 400 ml/acre.
  - High infestation: Spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP (Pegasus) @ 240 g/acre or Pyriproxyfen 10% EC @ 400-500 ml/acre or Dinotefuran 20% SG @ 60 g/acre.

## 4. Fertilizer Protocol for Cotton
- Rainfed cotton: 60:30:30 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.
- Irrigated/Bt Hybrid cotton: 120:60:60 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare.
- Split application: 20% N at sowing, 40% N at squaring (45 DAS), 40% N at peak flowering (75 DAS). Foliar spray of 1% KNO3 (Potassium Nitrate) twice during boll development reduces square drop.
