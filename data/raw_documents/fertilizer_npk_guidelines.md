# ICAR Technical Manual: Fertilizer Calculations, NPK Ratios & Soil Health

## 1. Primary Macronutrients & Standard Fertilizer Formulations
Fertilizer recommendations are universally given in pure nutrient terms:
- **N** (Elemental Nitrogen)
- **P2O5** (Available Phosphate / Phosphorus)
- **K2O** (Water-Soluble Potash / Potassium)

### Commercial Fertilizer Composition:
1. **Urea**: Contains 46% Nitrogen (N).
   - *Formula*: To provide $X$ kg of pure N, you require:
     $$\text{Urea required (kg)} = X \times \frac{100}{46} \approx X \times 2.174$$
2. **DAP (Di-Ammonium Phosphate)**: Contains 18% Nitrogen (N) and 46% Phosphorus ($P_2O_5$).
   - 100 kg DAP provides 18 kg N and 46 kg $P_2O_5$.
   - To provide $Y$ kg of $P_2O_5$ using DAP:
     $$\text{DAP required (kg)} = Y \times \frac{100}{46} \approx Y \times 2.174$$
   - *Note on N adjustment*: The DAP also supplies $N_{\text{supplied}} = \text{DAP} \times 0.18$. This quantity must be subtracted from the total Urea requirement.
3. **MOP (Muriate of Potash / Potassium Chloride)**: Contains 60% Potash ($K_2O$).
   - To provide $Z$ kg of $K_2O$:
     $$\text{MOP required (kg)} = Z \times \frac{100}{60} \approx Z \times 1.667$$
4. **SSP (Single Super Phosphate)**: Alternative to DAP. Contains 16% $P_2O_5$, 11-12% Sulphur (S), and 19-21% Calcium (Ca).
   - Ideal for oilseeds (mustard, groundnut) and pulses where sulphur is critical.
   - To provide $Y$ kg $P_2O_5$: $\text{SSP} = Y \times \frac{100}{16} = 6.25 \times Y$.

## 2. Standard Recommended Doses of Fertilizer (RDF) per Acre
Note: 1 Hectare = 2.471 Acres. To convert kg/ha to kg/acre, divide by 2.471 (or multiply by 0.405).

| Crop | RDF (N : P2O5 : K2O) kg/Acre | Typical Bags (50kg Urea, 50kg DAP, 50kg MOP) |
|---|---|---|
| **Rice (Paddy)** | 48 : 24 : 16 | ~1.7 bags Urea, 1 bag DAP, 0.5 bag MOP |
| **Wheat** | 48 : 24 : 16 | ~1.7 bags Urea, 1 bag DAP, 0.5 bag MOP |
| **Hybrid Maize** | 60 : 24 : 20 | ~2.2 bags Urea, 1 bag DAP, 0.67 bag MOP |
| **Bt Cotton** | 48 : 24 : 24 | ~1.7 bags Urea, 1 bag DAP, 0.8 bag MOP |
| **Sugarcane** | 100 : 45 : 45 | ~3.8 bags Urea, 2 bags DAP, 1.5 bags MOP |
| **Mustard / Rapeseed**| 32 : 16 : 16 (+ 10 kg Sulphur) | 1.1 bags Urea, 0.7 bag DAP (or 2.5 bags SSP), 0.5 bag MOP |
| **Gram / Chickpea** | 8 : 16 : 8 (Pulses fix atmospheric N) | 0.2 bag Urea, 0.7 bag DAP, 0.25 bag MOP |

## 3. Soil Test Calibration & Soil Health Card
Farmers should adjust RDF based on Soil Health Card fertility ratings:
- **Low Soil Fertility (<280 kg/ha available N, <10 kg/ha available P, <110 kg/ha available K)**:
  - Increase recommended dose by 25%.
- **Medium Soil Fertility (280-560 kg/ha N, 10-25 kg/ha P, 110-280 kg/ha K)**:
  - Apply 100% recommended dose.
- **High Soil Fertility (>560 kg/ha N, >25 kg/ha P, >280 kg/ha K)**:
  - Decrease recommended dose by 25%.

## 4. Micronutrient Applications
- **Zinc Deficiency**: Highly prevalent in Indian alluvial and calcareous soils. Apply 10 kg/acre Zinc Sulphate (21% Zn) as basal once every 2 years. Do not mix Zinc Sulphate directly with DAP in the same fertilizer tank, as it forms insoluble Zinc Phosphate precipitate.
- **Boron**: For cauliflower, tomato, sunflower and mustard, apply Borax @ 4 kg/acre to prevent hollow stem and flower drop.
