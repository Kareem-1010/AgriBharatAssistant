# Government of India Agricultural Welfare Schemes & Crop Insurance Guide

## 1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Objective**: Direct income support to landholding farmer families across India to meet agricultural and domestic financial requirements.
- **Financial Benefit**:
  - Rs 6,000 per year transferred directly into the verified bank account of the beneficiary farmer family.
  - Disbursed in **3 equal installments of Rs 2,000 every 4 months** (Period 1: April-July; Period 2: Aug-Nov; Period 3: Dec-March).
- **Eligibility & Mandatory Requirements**:
  - All landholding farmer families with cultivable land parcels registered in their names.
  - **Mandatory Linkages**: Aadhaar-seeded active bank account, completed e-KYC (via OTP on PM-KISAN portal or biometric at CSC center), and verified land record (Bhulekh / Land seeding).
- **Exclusion Criteria**:
  - Institutional landholders.
  - Farmer families where any member holds/held constitutional posts, serving/retired government employees (except Group D / multi-tasking staff), professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs), and individuals who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
  - Pensioners with monthly pension exceeding Rs 10,000.

## 2. PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)
- **Objective**: Comprehensive crop insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest.
- **Farmer Premium Rates (Highly Subsidized)**:
  - **Kharif Crops** (Paddy, Maize, Soybean, Groundnut, Cotton): Maximum **2.0%** of sum insured.
  - **Rabi Crops** (Wheat, Mustard, Barley, Gram): Maximum **1.5%** of sum insured.
  - **Annual Commercial & Horticultural Crops** (Sugarcane, Potato, Onion, Chilies, Banana): Maximum **5.0%** of sum insured.
  - Balance actuarial premium is shared 50:50 between the Central and State Governments (90:10 for North Eastern states).
- **Coverage Risks**:
  1. Prevented Sowing / Planting risk.
  2. Standing Crop loss (drought, dry spells, flood, pest & disease outbreak, hailstorm, cyclone).
  3. Post-Harvest losses (for crops kept in 'cut and spread' condition in the field for drying, up to 14 days after harvest due to unseasonal rains/cyclone).
  4. Localized Calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst).
- **Critical Claim Timelines**:
  - For localized calamities and post-harvest damage, the farmer must notify insurance within **72 hours** of loss occurrence through:
    * The 'Crop Insurance App' (NCIP).
    * Toll-Free Kisan Call Center: **1800-180-1551**.
    * Or written intimation to the local bank branch or Agricultural Officer.

## 3. Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme
- **Facility**: Provides timely, flexible, and simplified credit for crop cultivation and post-harvest expenses.
- **Interest Rate Subvention**:
  - Base interest rate is 9%. Government provides 2% Interest Subvention, making nominal interest 7% p.a.
  - **Prompt Repayment Incentive**: Additional 3% interest discount for farmers repaying within 1 year, bringing the **effective interest rate down to just 4.0% per annum** for loans up to Rs 3 Lakh.
  - Collateral-free agricultural loan limit increased to Rs 1.6 Lakh (up to Rs 3 Lakh with tie-up arrangements).
- Also extends to animal husbandry, dairy, and fisheries farmers with credit limit up to Rs 2 Lakh at subsidized rates.

## 4. Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme
- Issued every 3 years to individual farmers detailing the status of 12 nutritional parameters:
  - Macronutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K).
  - Secondary nutrient: Sulphur (S).
  - Micronutrients: Zinc (Zn), Iron (Fe), Copper (Cu), Manganese (Mn), Boron (Bo).
  - Physical parameters: pH, Electrical Conductivity (EC), Organic Carbon (OC).
- Accompanied by crop-wise customized fertilizer dosage recommendations to curb wasteful over-application of Urea.
