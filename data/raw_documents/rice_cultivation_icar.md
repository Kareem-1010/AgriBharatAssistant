# ICAR Comprehensive Package of Practices: Rice (Paddy) Cultivation

## 1. Agro-Climatic Requirements and Sowing
- **Season**: Kharif (major), Rabi/Boro (irrigated regions).
- **Optimum Temperature**: 22°C to 32°C. Temperatures above 35°C during flowering cause spikelet sterility.
- **Nursery Sowing**: May 15 to June 15 for wet nursery; transplanting at 20-25 days after sowing (DAS) with 2-3 seedlings per hill.
- **Spacing**: 20 cm x 15 cm for normal planting; 25 cm x 25 cm for System of Rice Intensification (SRI).

## 2. Water Management
- Maintain 2-3 cm shallow standing water during early vegetative stage (initial 7-10 days after transplanting).
- Adopt **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)** during active tillering to save 25-30% water and reduce methane emissions.
- Maintain 5 cm continuous ponding from panicle initiation to grain dough stage.
- Drain water completely 10-14 days before harvest.

## 3. Nutrient Management (NPK & Zinc)
- **Recommended Dose of Fertilizer (RDF)**: 120 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O per hectare (equivalent to 48 kg N : 24 kg P2O5 : 16 kg K2O per acre).
- **Application Schedule**:
  - Basal: 100% Phosphorus (DAP or SSP), 50% Potash (MOP), and 25% Nitrogen (Urea).
  - First Top-Dressing: 50% Nitrogen at active tillering (21-25 days after transplanting).
  - Second Top-Dressing: Remaining 25% Nitrogen and 50% Potash at panicle initiation (40-45 DAS).
- **Zinc Deficiency (Khaira Disease)**: Characterized by chlorosis at base of youngest leaves followed by brown rusty patches. Apply 25 kg/ha Zinc Sulphate (21%) as basal. If symptoms appear on standing crop, foliar spray 0.5% Zinc Sulphate (5 g/L) + 0.25% Lime (2.5 g/L) or Urea 1% in water twice at 7-day intervals.

## 4. Major Pests and ICAR IPM Management
### A. Yellow Stem Borer (*Scirpophaga incertulas*)
- **Symptoms**: 'Dead heart' in vegetative stage and 'White ear head' with chaffy grains during reproductive stage.
- **Economic Threshold Level (ETL)**: 10% dead hearts or 2 egg masses/sq.m or 1 moth/sq.m.
- **Control Measure**: 
  - Install pheromone traps with lure @ 3-5 traps/acre for monitoring, 10 traps/acre for mass trapping.
  - Apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 10 kg/acre or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen) @ 60 ml in 200 L water per acre, or Fipronil 5 SC @ 400 ml/acre.

### B. Brown Plant Hopper (BPH - *Nilaparvata lugens*)
- **Symptoms**: Circular patches of drying plants called 'Hopper burn' in the field center; black sooty mold on base.
- **ETL**: 5-10 hoppers per hill during vegetative stage; 15-20 hoppers per hill at heading.
- **Control Measure**:
  - Provide alleyways (30 cm paths every 2-3 meters) during transplanting for aeration and sunlight.
  - Avoid excessive use of nitrogenous fertilizers.
  - Spray Pymetrozine 50 WG (Chess) @ 120 g/acre or Triflumezopyrim 10% SC (Pexalon) @ 94 ml/acre directed towards the base of plants.

## 5. Major Diseases and Management
### A. Rice Blast (*Pyricularia oryzae*)
- **Symptoms**: Spindle-shaped eye spots with ash-grey center and brown margin on leaves (Leaf Blast); blackened nodes (Nodal Blast); broken neck (Neck Blast).
- **Control Measure**:
  - Seed treatment with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 2 g/kg seed.
  - Foliar spray of Tricyclazole 75 WP (Beam) @ 120 g/acre or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 300 ml/acre in 200 L water upon seeing initial lesions.

### B. Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB - *Xanthomonas oryzae*)
- **Symptoms**: Water-soaked lesions starting from leaf tips moving downwards along the margin with wavy edges, turning straw-yellow. Bacterial ooze beads seen on cut leaf in water.
- **Control Measure**:
  - Avoid excess urea and drain excess field water.
  - Foliar spray of Streptocycline @ 6 g + Copper Oxychloride 50 WP @ 300 g in 200 L water per acre. Repeat after 10-12 days if required.
