# ICAR & IISR Package of Practices: Sugarcane Cultivation & Red Rot Control

## 1. Planting Windows and Varieties
- **Autumn Planting**: October to November (higher sugar recovery and 15-20% higher cane yield).
- **Spring Planting**: February to March.
- **Adsali Planting**: July to August (prevalent in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu; 15-18 month duration).
- **Recommended Red Rot Resistant Varieties**: Co 0118, Co 15023, CoLk 14201, Co 98014. Note: Susceptible variety Co 0238 has suffered severe Red Rot epidemics in Northern India and farmers are advised to replace it.

## 2. Red Rot Disease (*Colletotrichum falcatum*)
- **Significance**: Termed 'Cancer of Sugarcane'. Causes total crop collapse.
- **Symptoms**:
  - Yellowing and drying of the 3rd or 4th leaf from top, progressing downward.
  - Splitting the cane lengthwise reveals blood-red discoloration of internal tissues interspersed with characteristic **white transverse patches** across the red zones.
  - Acidic or alcoholic sour fermentation odor from freshly split affected canes.
- **Management**:
  - Use healthy, certified, certified seed setts free from disease.
  - Sett treatment: Soak two-bud setts in Carbendazim 50 WP (1 g per 1 L water) for 15 minutes before planting, or moist hot air treatment (54°C for 2.5 hours).
  - Rogue out and burn infected clumps immediately. Do not ratoon infected crops.
  - Practice crop rotation with paddy or green manure (Dhaincha/Sunn hemp).

## 3. Water Management & Drip Irrigation
- Sugarcane is a high water-consuming crop, requiring 1,500 to 2,500 mm water across its crop cycle.
- **Drip Irrigation**:
  - Paired-row planting (30:120:30 cm) with inline drip tubing delivers water directly to root zones.
  - Saves 45-50% irrigation water and improves fertilizer efficiency through fertigation.
- **Irrigation Interval**:
  - Tillering/Formative phase (March-June): Critical water-stress period, irrigate every 7-10 days in conventional furrow.
  - Grand growth phase (Monsoon): Ensure good field drainage; water stagnation causes rooting decay.

## 4. Nutrient Schedule
- Recommended RDF: 250 kg N : 115 kg P2O5 : 115 kg K2O per hectare.
- Basal: Full P2O5 and 50% K2O with 25% Nitrogen.
- Top-Dressing: Nitrogen applied in three split doses at 45, 90, and 120 DAS. Complete all nitrogen application before final earthing up (120 DAS) to prevent late tillering and watery canes.
