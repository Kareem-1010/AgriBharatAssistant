# ICAR-IIHR Guidelines: Tomato & Vegetable Disease Management

## 1. Early Blight vs. Late Blight in Tomato & Potato (Diagnosis & Control)

### A. Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*)
- **Occurrence**: Warm, alternating wet and dry conditions (temp 24°C-30°C).
- **Diagnostic Symptoms**:
  - Starts on older lower leaves as dark brown to black circular spots.
  - Development of characteristic **concentric rings forming a 'target board' pattern**.
  - Chlorotic yellow halo surrounds the concentric rings. Leads to leaf defoliation from bottom upward.
- **Control**:
  - Preventative foliar spray: Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L water or Chlorothalonil 75 WP @ 2 g/L water.
  - Curative spray at onset of lesions: Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 ml/L OR Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/L in water.

### B. Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*)
- **Occurrence**: Cool, highly humid, cloudy weather with morning fog (temp 10°C-22°C, relative humidity >90%). Destroys foliage within 3-5 days.
- **Diagnostic Symptoms**:
  - Water-soaked pale green to dark brown lesions at leaf tips or margins.
  - In humid mornings, a delicate **white downy fungal growth (frosty mildew)** is clearly visible on the underside of diseased leaves.
  - Dark brown sunken firm rot on green tomato fruits and potato tubers.
- **Control**:
  - Preventative: Copper Oxychloride 50 WP @ 2.5-3.0 g/L or Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Curative / Severe Outbreak: Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold) @ 2.5 g/L OR Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1 g/L + Mancozeb 2 g/L OR Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Curzate) @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Spray interval: 7-10 days depending on weather humidity.

## 2. Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV)
- **Vector**: Silverleaf Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*).
- **Symptoms**: Severe upward curling, twisting and crinkling of leaves; reduced leaf size; thick veins; bushy stunted plant stature; severe fruit drop.
- **Management Protocol**:
  - Seedlings raised under nylon insect nets (40-50 mesh) to avoid early vector contact.
  - Install yellow sticky traps @ 20 traps/acre.
  - Spray vector insecticides: Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 ml/L OR Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L OR Cyantraniliprole 10.26% OD @ 1.8 ml/L.
  - Remove and destroy virus-infected plants early to curb field transmission.

## 3. Tomato Blossom End Rot (Physiological Disorder)
- **Symptom**: Water-soaked lesion on the blossom end of the fruit turning dark sunken and leathery.
- **Cause**: Calcium deficiency exacerbated by irregular soil moisture or excessive nitrogen.
- **Correction**: Maintain even irrigation; foliar spray of Calcium Nitrate @ 2-3 g/L twice at fruit expansion stage.
