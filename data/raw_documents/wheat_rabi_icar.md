# ICAR Comprehensive Package of Practices: Wheat Cultivation (Rabi Season)

## 1. Sowing Time and Seed Rate
- **Timely Sown Irrigated**: First fortnight of November (Nov 1 to Nov 15) is optimum. Seed rate: 100 kg/ha (40 kg/acre).
- **Late Sown Irrigated**: Nov 16 to Dec 15. Seed rate increased to 125 kg/ha (50 kg/acre) due to reduced tillering period.
- **Line Sowing**: Depth 4-5 cm using seed-cum-fertilizer drill with row-to-row spacing of 20-22.5 cm.

## 2. Critical Irrigation Stages (Crown Root Initiation is Vital)
Wheat requires 4 to 6 irrigations depending on soil type. If water is limited, prioritize according to following critical stages:
1. **Crown Root Initiation (CRI)**: 20-25 days after sowing (DAS). **Most critical stage**; skipping this reduces yield by 30-40%.
2. **Tillering Stage**: 40-45 DAS.
3. **Late Jointing Stage**: 60-65 DAS.
4. **Flowering Stage**: 80-85 DAS.
5. **Milking / Grain Development**: 100-105 DAS.
6. **Dough Stage**: 115-120 DAS.

*Caution*: Do not irrigate during high winds at late grain-filling stage to prevent lodging.

## 3. Nutrient Management (NPK & Splits)
- **Timely Sown Irrigated**: 120 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O per hectare (48 kg N : 24 kg P2O5 : 16 kg K2O per acre).
- **High-Yielding / Late Sown**: 150 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Split Application**:
  - Basal at sowing: 1/3rd Nitrogen (Urea) + 100% Phosphorus (DAP or SSP) + 100% Potassium (MOP).
  - First Top-Dressing: 1/3rd Nitrogen at 1st irrigation (CRI stage, 21-25 DAS).
  - Second Top-Dressing: Remaining 1/3rd Nitrogen at 2nd irrigation (Tillering/Jointing, 40-45 DAS).

## 4. Weed Management (Phalaris minor / Gullidanda)
- **Problem**: Little seed canary grass (*Phalaris minor*) and wild oats (*Avena ludoviciana*), broad-leaved weeds (*Chenopodium album* - Bathua).
- **Chemical Control**:
  - For mixed weed flora at 30-35 DAS: Spray Sulfosulfuron 75% WG @ 13.5 g/acre in 150 L water.
  - For grassy weeds (Phalaris minor): Spray Clodinafop-propargyl 15% WP @ 160 g/acre or Pinoxaden 5.1% EC @ 400 ml/acre.
  - For broadleaf weeds only: 2,4-D amine salt 58% SL @ 400 ml/acre or Metsulfuron methyl 20% WP @ 8 g/acre.
  - Ensure spraying is done with flat-fan nozzle when soil has sufficient moisture.

## 5. Major Diseases and Management
### A. Yellow (Stripe) Rust (*Puccinia striiformis*)
- **Symptoms**: Yellow powdery pustules arranged in parallel stripes/lines on leaves, staining fingers with yellow powder on touch. Prevalent in North-Western plains in cool humid weather (Jan-Feb).
- **Control Measure**:
  - Grow resistant cultivars (e.g., HD 3086, PBW 725, DBW 187, DBW 222).
  - At first appearance of yellow stripes, spray Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 200 ml in 200 L water per acre or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 200 ml/acre. Repeat after 15 days if rust spreads.

### B. Karnal Bunt (*Neovossia indica*)
- **Symptoms**: Partial conversion of wheat grain into black foul-smelling powder (fishy odor due to trimethylamine).
- **Control Measure**: Single foliar spray of Propiconazole 25% EC @ 200 ml/acre at earhead emergence stage if humid cloudy weather prevails.
